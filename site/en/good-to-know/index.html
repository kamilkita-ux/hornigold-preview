FAQ

Good to know

Clear answers before you choose your stay.

Where is Hornigold?

Hornigold w Zielonej Kamienicy, Kopernika 6, 40-064 Katowice.

How can I contact Hornigold?

+48 608 662 707 · office@hornigold.pl

How do I walk from Katowice station?

Leave via plac Oddziałów Młodzieży Powstańczej. Follow Andrzeja and Marii Skłodowskiej-Curie to Kopernika 6. Directions and panoramas are on the Location page.

What are the breakfast hours?

Daily, 7:00–10:00.

Does Hornigold have parking?

Hornigold has private parking for a stay in central Katowice. Hornigold is at Kopernika 6. Contact reception about parking for your car and arrival arrangements; the Location page provides routes to Hornigold.

Which wellness services are available?

After a day in Katowice, make time to relax at Hornigold. Our wellness services include a sauna, hot tub and massages. They can complement a day of meetings or a leisurely city weekend. Contact reception to arrange a time and discuss the service you would like.

Can I arrange a business meeting?

Combine a meeting in central Katowice with a stay at the Green Townhouse. Hornigold offers the Madlen, Camila and Ana meeting spaces, measuring 40, 60 and 100 m². In your enquiry, include the date, number of guests, preferred seating layout and any accommodation or catering requirements so we can discuss your event.

Do the rooms have Wi-Fi?

Wi-Fi is listed among the amenities in all nine room and apartment categories.

Is air conditioning available in every room?

Air conditioning is listed for Deluxe Premium, Apartament Deluxe, Prestige Deluxe and Hornigold. Please check the amenities of your chosen category.

Can I compare rooms before choosing?

Yes. Compare up to three categories by floor area, beds, amenities and starting prices.

Which apartment has the largest floor area?

The Hornigold apartment measures 105 m² and has two bedrooms, two kitchenettes and two bathrooms.

Where can I find the walk from the station to Hornigold?

On the Location page, use the railway station section to open walking directions or the virtual walk. The photograph of the entrance with flags helps identify the building.

Specific needs? Write to us — we’ll help you choose a room.

+48 608 662 707 office@hornigold.pl

Essential information before you arrive

Does submitting an enquiry confirm a reservation?

No. Preparing an enquiry does not check availability, hold a room or collect payment. Agree the price, availability and booking confirmation process with reception.

Stay enquiry
Is a ‘from’ price the price of my stay?

A ‘from’ price does not replace a quote for your dates and number of guests. Ask for the total amount, what it includes, any additional charges and the cancellation conditions.

Contact
Where can I check the conditions before confirming a stay?

Open the Terms and documents page. Read the documents about your stay and the processing of personal data; clarify any questions with reception before agreeing the conditions.
