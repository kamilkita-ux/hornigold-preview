Premium is a category at Hornigold in the Green Townhouse, at Kopernika 6 in Katowice. Its floor area is 35 m². Bed arrangement: One double bed. Amenities include Wi-Fi; Kitchenette; Whirlpool bath and shower. Use these details to choose a space that fits the way you plan to spend your time, whether you are visiting the city for a short break or combining work and leisure.

Explore the gallery before choosing. The photographs include Bedroom with a large window and greenery outside. They show individual interiors in this category. Look at the layout, the space around the bed and the bathroom fittings. If a particular feature matters to your stay, mention it in your enquiry so reception can help match the room to your needs.

The comparison tool lets you view this category alongside two others, including floor area, beds, amenities and starting prices. Your selected dates and guest count are retained while you browse. The total price, availability and rate conditions depend on an offer for your specific dates. An enquiry to reception is not a reservation.

Before travelling, visit the Location page for routes from the railway station and airports and a photograph of the entrance with flags. Breakfast is served from 7:00 to 10:00. Sauna, hot tub and massage services are also available; discuss your preferred service with reception.
