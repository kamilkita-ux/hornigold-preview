What your quote should include

Include dates, numbers of adults and children, bed arrangement and room count in your enquiry. If stairs, bathroom layout, workspace or parking matter to you, describe this before choosing a category. Wait for the property to confirm the terms.

Ask for the total price for your dates and party, the category and bed arrangement, payment and cancellation terms, and separate costs for breakfast, parking and extras. Compare complete offers for the same dates. An enquiry does not hold a room.
