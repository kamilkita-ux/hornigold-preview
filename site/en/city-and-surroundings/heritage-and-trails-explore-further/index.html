Beyond the city

Heritage and trails: explore further

A guide is a beginning, not the whole region in a few chapters. Here is a way into broader documentation and official catalogues.

History and context

The National Institute of Cultural Heritage’s Zabytek.pl provides descriptions, documentation and a heritage map. Use its place and object-type filters. For themed visits, also consult the Industrial Monuments, Eagles’ Nests and Wooden Architecture routes. A heritage register is not a list of sites open to visitors.

Take a closer look

When looking for a place, compare its name, address and custodian. Check the site manager’s rules before planning to enter. Our articles are an editorial selection; broader research continues through the official resources below, not through a claim that every Silesian monument is already described here.

Before your visit

Check hours, tickets, access, accessibility and guided-tour languages with the site’s custodian. The external sources below may not be available in your chosen language.
