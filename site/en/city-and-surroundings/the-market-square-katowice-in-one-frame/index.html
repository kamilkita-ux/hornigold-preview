Katowice up close

The market square: Katowice in one frame

Begin your conversation with the city here. Rather than a uniform old town, look for different ideas of modern life meeting in one place.

History and context

The square changed with Katowice, including major alterations in the 1960s and 1970s. Around it stand the Silesian Theatre and buildings from different periods. It is a useful starting point for understanding why central Katowice looks unlike the medieval market squares of many Polish towns.

Take a closer look

Pause to compare window rhythms, rooflines and the scale of buildings. Then choose one direction: along 3 Maja Street or towards the Culture Zone. There is no need to see everything at once; a few carefully observed details can stay with you longer than a hurried checklist.

Before your visit

Check hours, tickets, access, accessibility and guided-tour languages with the site’s custodian. The external sources below may not be available in your chosen language.
