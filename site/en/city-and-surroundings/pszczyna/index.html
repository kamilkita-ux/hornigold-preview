Beyond the city

Pszczyna: a residence and a gentler pace

After industrial stories, change perspective. In Pszczyna, consider how display, domestic life and landscape belong together.

History and context

The residence owes much of its present character to the 1870–1876 remodelling designed by Alexandre-Hippolyte Destailleur. The Castle Museum introduces its interiors and aristocratic history. Also consider the palace as a workplace for people who rarely appear in formal portraits.

Take a closer look

Avoid packing too many museums into one day. Leave time to step outside, walk and discuss what caught your attention. Check exhibition choices, tickets and accessibility directly with the museum.

Before your visit

Check hours, tickets, access, accessibility and guided-tour languages with the site’s custodian. The external sources below may not be available in your chosen language.
