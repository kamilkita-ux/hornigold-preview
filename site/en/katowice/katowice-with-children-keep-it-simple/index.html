Katowice with children: keep it simple

For adults planning a relaxed day with children.

One activity is enough

Choose an event or workshop from the current Silesian Museum programme. Check age limits and whether your group must book ahead.

Allow breaks

Plan food and rest between journeys. Adapt outdoor activities to the weather and children’s needs.

Confirm room suitability

Tell reception how many adults and children are travelling and the children’s ages. Confirm beds, extra-bed options and all supplements before booking.

If the weather changes

Choose an indoor activity suitable for the children’s ages; check the programme and places with the organiser.

Giszowiec — the garden-city idea

Low houses, pitched roofs and abundant greenery evoke a garden settlement. Giszowiec was built in 1907–1910. Today’s neighbourhood combines surviving early buildings with later changes.
