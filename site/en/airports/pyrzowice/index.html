Airports and transfers

Katowice–Pyrzowice Airport (KTW)

Where is the airport?

Katowice Airport is in Pyrzowice, north of Katowice and outside the city centre. Its address is Wolności 90, 42-625 Ożarowice. Select the KTW passenger terminal in navigation, not the management office in Katowice or Kraków–Balice Airport.

What does the terminal look like?

The photographs show the entrance to Katowice Airport and the interior of Terminal B. Open the official map to locate your terminal, check-in, arrivals and parking. The virtual tour helps you explore the space beforehand. Follow current signs and airline information; a photograph is not a travel plan.

Official terminal mapOfficial virtual tour

By car or taxi

Start at Hornigold, Kopernika 6, and set the Katowice Airport terminal as your destination. Navigation will choose a route to Pyrzowice based on current traffic. Before entering, check drop-off and parking signs and fees. Book a taxi or transfer separately, confirming the total fare and meeting point.

Official transport information

By bus and train

Bus AP links Katowice with the airport. Check the current stop, departure and ticket in the official ZTM timetable. For rail, search for “Pyrzowice Lotnisko” and choose a connection from Katowice for your travel date, without assuming a fixed number of changes. The airport states that a roughly 500 m footpath links the station and terminals; allow for luggage and weather.

Trains — airport information

Before you leave

Check the airport and terminal on your ticket. Add the airline’s recommended check-in time, security checks and a buffer for disruption to your journey time. For a night flight, check the first or last service. Arrange reduced-mobility assistance with your airline and airport in advance.
