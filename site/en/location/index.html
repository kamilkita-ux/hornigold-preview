Virtual walk from the station to Hornigold

Get to know the way before you arrive. Three verified street panoramas guide you from the area by the station’s southern exit to the entrance to Hornigold.

Start at Katowice station

Head for the southern exit onto plac Oddziałów Młodzieży Powstańczej, towards Andrzeja Street. This tour has no panoramas inside the station. The mapped route includes stairs; with a wheelchair, pushchair or heavy luggage, check available exits and lifts at the station.
